The short answer

Express Cuts is a unisex salon at 25/2, Ayyappa Nagar Main Rd, Priyadarshini Layout, Krishnarajapuram, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560037, about 2 KM from Hoodi Circle. It opens every day, and walk-ins are welcome. You can also call +91 89700 00135 or message on WhatsApp before you come.

Where is Express Cuts?

The salon is on Ayyappa Nagar Main Road in Priyadarshini Layout, Krishnarajapuram, Bengaluru, roughly 2 KM from Hoodi Circle. The full address is 25/2, Ayyappa Nagar Main Rd, Priyadarshini Layout, Krishnarajapuram, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560037. The quickest way to find it is to open the Google Maps listing.

What are the opening hours?

  • Monday – Friday: 08:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Saturday – Sunday: 07:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Do you need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome. If you would rather plan ahead, message the salon on WhatsApp or call +91 89700 00135 to check a suitable time.

What services can you get?

Express Cuts covers everyday grooming and longer treatments. Each service has its own page, with more detail on what is available:

  • Haircuts and styling, from classic cuts to fades and tapers
  • Beard grooming, including trims, line-ups and clean shaves
  • Facials, including Type 1 to Type 4 facials and the Hydra Facial
  • Hair spa, with cleansing, massage and treatment
  • Hair colour, including grey coverage, highlights and streaks

What do offers and packages cost?

Prices are published on the offers page, which always has the current list. At the time of writing, a men's haircut is ₹99, and the Classic Combo of a haircut, beard trim and head massage is ₹249.

How do you get the most from a first visit?

  1. Bring a photo or two of the look you want.
  2. Allow enough time, particularly if you are planning a facial, hair spa or hair colour. Ask how long to set aside when you message.
  3. Tell the team about any skin sensitivity or previous reactions to hair dye.
  4. Ask how to maintain the style at home before you leave.

What is the salon like?

You can see the reception, styling floor and wash area in the salon gallery, and read how Express Cuts began in our story. The salon has been running in KR Puram since 2021.

In short

  • Express Cuts is on Ayyappa Nagar Main Road, about 2 KM from Hoodi Circle.
  • The salon opens every day, and walk-ins are welcome.
  • Call or WhatsApp if you would like to check a time first.
  • Current prices are always on the offers page.

Related questions

Is Express Cuts a unisex salon?

Yes. Express Cuts is a unisex salon. This website focuses on men's grooming, so please contact the salon to ask about any other service you are looking for.

Is the salon open on weekends?

Yes. It opens from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM on Saturdays and Sundays.

Is there parking at the salon?

Please contact the salon for current parking information before you visit.
